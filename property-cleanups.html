When people call me for this

When the outside needs a reset.

  • The yard got away from them over the summer and it's knee high.
  • A storm came through and the yard is full of branches.
  • They're selling the house and it needs to look right for photos.
  • They're renting the place out and the last tenant left it rough.
  • Code enforcement sent a letter.
  • A ficus, mango or oak dropped half of itself on the lawn.

What a cleanup covers

Cutting down overgrowth, trimming back hedges and vines that have taken over, clearing landscape beds, raking and bagging, dragging branches to the curb for pickup, and hauling off what the county won't take.

We leave it mowed and edged, not just cleared.

How cleanups get priced

By the job, not by the hour.

Two overgrown yards are never the same, and I'd rather quote you a flat number than have you watching the clock while we work.

Text me a couple of photos and I'll get you that number the same day.
